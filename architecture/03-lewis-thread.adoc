== Role of C. S. Lewis

Lewis is not a separate chapter dump.

He is a guide woven through the whole project.

Scripture remains the authority.

Lewis helps name,
imagine,
and illuminate the realities under discussion.

== Nonfiction Stream

Lewis's nonfiction helps identify and analyze
the phenomena.

Representative works:

* Mere Christianity
* Miracles
* The Abolition of Man
* Surprised by Joy
* The Weight of Glory
* The Four Loves
* Letters to Malcolm

== Fiction Stream

Lewis's fiction helps readers imaginatively
inhabit the phenomena.

Representative works:

* The Chronicles of Narnia
* Out of the Silent Planet
* Perelandra
* That Hideous Strength
* The Great Divorce
* Till We Have Faces
