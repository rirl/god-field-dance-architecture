== Governing Metaphor

The Grand Dance represents the participatory
destination of the project.

It gathers:

* beauty,
* harmony,
* order,
* vocation,
* love,
* joy,
* communion,
* and participation.

----

== Theological Guardrail

The Grand Dance must remain subordinate to
biblical interpretation.

It is a metaphor,
not an independent doctrine.

It helps readers imagine the invitation
toward participation in the life of God.
